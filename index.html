Frequently asked
questions

Can I join BeyondPass today?

BeyondPass is a proposed service. The R$159.90 monthly price is an estimate from the original pitch.

Which cities would it be available in?

The launch cities and listings would depend on local partnerships.

Do I have to use all four monthly bookings?

You’d have up to four bookings each month and could use as many as you like within that limit.

How would the AI recommendations work?

The idea is to recommend experiences based on the interests you share. The filter above gives you a preview.
